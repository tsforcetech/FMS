## Staff identity design

1. The trusted operator provisions a login with a staff role (`provision-staff.mjs`: Admin SDK, refuses owner/admin, prints a one-time password-setup link, writes the membership and directory entry, audits).
2. An owner/admin links that login to one employee in **Administration → Staff access** and chooses the sites (≤10) or all sites. The transaction writes `staffLinks/{uid}` and the claim `employeeLinks/{employeeId}`; the rules require an active membership, an active employee and the matching claim, so a login cannot represent two employees and an employee cannot have two logins.
3. On sign-in the staff app reads `users/{uid}/companyAccess`, then for each company resolves membership → link → employee. Staff never choose an identity. A missing link, revoked link, inactive employee or inactive membership shows a clear denial screen.
4. Revoking the link, suspending the employee or deactivating the membership removes access immediately (every rule checks all three).
5. Multiple companies: each company has its own link; the profile screen switches company and clears cached labels.

## Permissions

Existing roles are reused (no new role model). Technicians/staff work on tasks assigned to them; supervisors, department and site managers additionally see and review work in their scope sites. See the access matrix in [phase5-schema.md](database/phase5-schema.md). Notable decisions:

- Assigned staff can **read** their tasks even after a site is removed from scope (history), but cannot **execute** them until the site is restored. Supervisors lose visibility immediately.
- Staff lists use queries the rules can prove (`assigneeIds array-contains <my employee>`, `siteId in <scope>`); nothing downloads company collections and filters in the browser.
- Timeline events, submissions and decisions are read one document at a time by ID for visible tasks.
- No staff member is made an admin anywhere.

## Staff Web

- Separate Vite app, mobile-first: bottom navigation (Home, My tasks, Report, Alerts, Profile) on phones, side rail from 960 px. Reuses the audited admin data layer (`@admin` alias) and the Phase 3 checklist engine with the task's frozen snapshot.
- **My Day**: greeting, date, primary site, today's shift from shift assignments (or leave/holiday), six live counters, “Start next task” (order: overdue high/critical, in progress, rework, overdue, high priority, due time), today's cards.
- **Checklist**: one section per step with progress, conditional questions, inline range/required validation, Save progress (revision-checked, stale copies rejected), unsaved-change warnings, review summary with failed checks and pending evidence, submit for approval (or complete when no approval is needed). Submitted attempts are immutable.
- **Rework**: banner with the reviewer's reason, previous answers, “Start correction” creates the next attempt.
- **Report a problem**: from a task (site/location/asset carried forward, optional question, optional repair request) or standalone in a scope site. IDs are generated once per form, so retries never duplicate; confirmation shows the reference.
- **Work orders**: assigned and self-reported; start, hold, resume, add update, request assistance (notifies reviewers), resolve. Verify/close stay with supervisors/admins. Materials remain a store/supervisor ledger action.
- Offline banner; buttons disabled while saving; permission errors explained in plain language. No company data is stored in browser storage (only the language preference and current company ID in session storage).

## Limitations

- The supervisor reviews in either app; approvals by supervisors rely on the site scope, not on a separate approval matrix.
- Notifications to a specific supervisor are not targeted by site (owners/admins and staff-role reviewers are notified).
- Translations other than English cover the main flow only and are labelled preview.
- Phase 6 (native apps, offline sync, photo/QR/GPS evidence, attendance) has not been started.
