## 0.8 — 15 November 2026

- **Excel, PDF and CSV export** on every list and report, with filters, column choice, progress and
  no silent truncation ([Exports](article:exports.overview)).
- **Import Center** for master data and opening stock, with templates, dry-run validation, error
  reports and safe resume ([Import](article:import.overview)).
- **Company setup** wizard with progress and **starter packs** for seven business types
  ([Company setup](article:setup.wizard)).
- **Plans and subscriptions**: **Administration → Plan & usage** shows limits, usage, features,
  billing history and support-access requests ([Plan & usage](article:saas.plan-usage)).
- **Invitations** by email and role, accepted with a verified email ([Invitations](article:setup.invitations)).
- The Sharaco platform console for Sharaco staff.

![Starter pack selection in Company setup](screens/phase8-01-onboarding-starter-pack.png)

## 0.7 — 15 November 2026

Reports & analytics with the **Management dashboard**, the **customer portal**, compliance records,
maintenance costs, audit history and backup status ([Management dashboard](article:reports.dashboard),
[Customer requests](article:customer.requests)).

## 0.6 — 15 November 2026

The Android/iOS **mobile app** with QR scanning, photos, GPS, attendance and offline work
([Install the mobile app](article:mobile.install), [Offline work](article:mobile.offline)).

## 0.5 — 14 November 2026

The **Staff web** portal for daily work: My Day, tasks, checklists, rework, problem reports, work
orders, calendar, notifications and supervisor approvals ([My Day](article:staff.my-day)).

## 0.4 — 14 November 2026

Recurring scheduling, task calendar, preventive maintenance, work orders, complaints, escalations, SLA
monitoring and notifications ([Recurring tasks](article:scheduling.recurring)).

## 0.3 — 14 November 2026

Task templates with checklist builder, RACI and publishing; task execution and approvals
([Template builder](article:templates.builder)).

## 0.2 — 14 November 2026

People and teams, assets and transfers, materials, stores and the stock ledger.

## 0.1

Company profile, clients, contracts, sites, locations and facilities ([Create a site](article:sites.create)).

## Still pending activation

These are built but depend on infrastructure or approvals that are not in place yet. They are described
as such throughout this Help Center:

- Automatic task scheduler runs (use **Run scheduler now** meanwhile).
- Online payments; invoices are recorded manually by Sharaco.
- Email delivery of invitations and reminders; links are shared manually.
- Automatic subscription expiry and usage reconciliation (run by Sharaco operators).
- Automatic backups and data purging.
- Arabic text shaping in PDF exports (use Excel).
